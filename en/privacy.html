Privacy Policy

Tsumiage does not collect any of your information.
Last updated: October 11, 2026

What is stored

  • Your goals, entries (numbers, notes and dates) and display settings are stored only on your iPhone. Home Screen widgets read the same data on the same iPhone.
  • There is no developer server and no account.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Features we use

  • Notifications: only when you turn on the daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and nothing is sent out.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: only when you turn on the app lock. iOS checks it's you, and the app never receives face or fingerprint data.
  • CSV export: only when you choose to export, your entries are saved to a file and handed to the share sheet.
  • Purchases: Tsumiage Pro is processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

What we don't collect

No ads, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting tools. The app does not connect to the internet, except for talking to the App Store when you buy.
