Privacy Policy

Tsumiage does not collect any of your information.
Last updated: November 14, 2026

What is stored

  • Your goals, entries (numbers, notes and dates) and display settings are stored on your iPhone. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the same data on the same iPhone.
  • If you turn on backup, a copy is saved to your own iCloud Drive, in the iCloud of your Apple Account. The developer cannot see it.
  • There is no developer server and no account.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data on your iPhone. An iCloud Drive backup stays until you delete it.

Features we use

  • Notifications: only when you turn on a reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and nothing is sent out.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: only when you turn on the app lock. iOS checks it's you, and the app never receives face or fingerprint data.
  • iCloud Drive: only when you turn on backup, to save a copy of your entries to your iCloud Drive and read it back when you restore.
  • CSV export and import: only when you choose to, your entries are saved to a file and handed to the share sheet, or read from a file you pick.
  • Purchases: Tsumiage Pro is processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

What we don't collect

No ads, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting tools. The app never sends information to the developer. It only uses the internet for App Store purchases and, when backup is on, iCloud Drive (an Apple service).
