FAQ

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen → “Edit” → “Add Widget” → “Tsumiage”. The medium size has buttons that log with one tap. To choose the goal and chart, touch and hold the widget → “Edit Widget”. You can also add Tsumiage to the Lock Screen and Control Center.

I logged the wrong number

Right after logging, tap “Undo” at the top. Later, open the goal’s entry list and swipe left on an entry to edit or delete it.

How do I log money I spent?

In the log sheet, switch from saving to spending and enter the amount. It is saved as a withdrawal and subtracted from the total.

How many goals can I have?

Without Pro you can have 3 active goals at a time. Tsumiage Pro (one-time purchase) makes it unlimited.

I bought Pro but it isn’t active

Open Settings → “Restore purchase”. Other iPhones with the same Apple Account get Pro at no extra cost. Family Sharing is supported.

Which currency and distance unit does the app use?

They follow your iPhone’s region: dollars, won, euros or yen, and miles or kilometers.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

With backup on, Tsumiage saves a copy to your own iCloud Drive every day. Install the app on the new iPhone with the same Apple Account and restore from the backup. You can also import a CSV file.

What happens if I delete the app?

Goals and entries on the iPhone are deleted. If you have an iCloud Drive backup, you can restore it after reinstalling. Without a backup they can’t be recovered.
