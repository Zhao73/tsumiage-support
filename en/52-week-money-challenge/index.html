52-week money challenge: how it works, plus a printable chart

Save a little more each week for a year. Tick a square every time you save and you can see how far you have left at a glance.

Key facts

  • The 52-week money challenge: save $1 in week 1, $2 in week 2, and keep adding $1 a week.
  • Week 52 is $52. The total after 52 weeks is $1,378.
  • The maths: $1 × (1 + 2 + … + 52) = $1 × 1,378 = $1,378.
  • Scale it up: $5 steps give $6,890, $10 steps give $13,780.
  • Reverse order or any order you like: the total is still $1,378.

How the 52-week challenge works

  1. Get a chart. Use 52 squares from $1 to $52 — print the PDF below or pick the 52-week challenge in the app.
  2. Pick one square each week. Put that amount in a jar or a savings account.
  3. Tick the square. Each tick adds to your running total.
  4. Finish all 52. At $1 steps you have $1,378.

Total = $1 × (1 + 2 + … + 52) = $1 × 52 × 53 ÷ 2 = $1,378

Four ways to play

Fill all 52 squares and the total is the same whichever way you go.

In order

$1, $2, $3… Easy to start; December brings the biggest weeks, right when spending peaks. Plan for it.

Reverse

Start at $52 and go down. You do the hard part while motivation is high, and the holidays get easier.

Any order

Pick the square that fits this week: a big one after payday, a small one in a tight week.

Random

Let chance pick one of the remaining squares. The app can pick a random open square for you.

Tips to finish

  • Pick a start date: January 1, a birthday or a payday. Whatever week you start is week 1.
  • In order, the halfway point (week 26) is only $351 — about 25% of the total. Knowing the back half is heavier helps you plan.
  • Save on the same day every week. With a bank account, move the money on payday.
  • Missed a week? Pick a small square or do two next week. Not stopping is what matters.
  • Keep the chart where you see it. In the app, a Home Screen widget shows your progress.

Questions

How much does the 52-week money challenge save?

$1,378 at $1 steps. At $5 steps it is $6,890 and at $10 steps $13,780.

Can I start in the middle of the year?

Yes. The week you start is week 1. After 52 weeks the total is the same.

What if I miss a week?

Pick a smaller square or save two squares next week. Order does not matter, so the total stays the same.

Is there a version without dollars?

Yes. The blank PDF has week numbers 1–52 only. Multiply each number by your own step in any currency.
