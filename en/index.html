FAQ

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen → “Edit” → “Add Widget” → “Tsumiage”. The medium size has buttons that log with one tap. To choose the goal and chart, touch and hold the widget → “Edit Widget”.

I logged the wrong number

Right after logging, tap “Undo” at the top. Later, open the goal's entry list and tap or swipe left on an entry to edit or delete it.

How many goals can I have?

The free version holds 2 active goals at a time. Tsumiage Pro (one-time purchase) makes it unlimited.

I bought Pro but it isn't active

Open Settings → “Restore purchase”. Other iPhones with the same Apple Account get Pro at no extra cost. Family Sharing is supported.

Which currency and distance unit does the app use?

They follow your iPhone's region: dollars, won, euros or yen, and miles or kilometers.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your data is stored only on your iPhone. Restoring a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings your entries along. You can also export each goal as CSV (Pro).

What happens if I delete the app?

All goals and entries are deleted and can't be recovered.
