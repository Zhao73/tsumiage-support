Tsumiage for iPhone

Save it up, one square at a time.

Tsumiage is an iPhone app for savings challenges — the 52-week challenge, the 100 envelope challenge, the 365-day challenge and more — and for your own savings goals. Set a number, then stack up what you save with one tap.

Three steps

  1. Type a name, get a goal

    Write something like Hawaii trip $2,000 by December and the type, target and deadline fill in, with how much to save per day.

  2. Log with one tap

    From Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, Shortcuts or Siri — without opening the app.

  3. Watch it stack up

    Charts show your progress, medals and badges mark your streaks. Money you spend is logged as a withdrawal.

Questions

More how-to answers are on the support page.

Can I use it without buying Pro?

Yes. Without Pro you get 3 active goals, every savings challenge, widgets and backup. Tsumiage Pro is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited goals and more. There is no subscription. The price depends on your country and is shown in the App Store.

Are there ads?

No. Never while you log, never when you open the app.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iPhones with iOS 26 or later.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and, if you turn on backup, in your own iCloud Drive. Nothing is sent to the developer. No account needed.

Can I move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Tsumiage backs up to your iCloud Drive every day, so you can restore after switching phones or reinstalling. CSV import is supported too.

What about money I take out, or a wrong entry?

Log money you spend as a withdrawal. Entries can be edited or deleted later, and undone right after logging.

Can I track things other than money?

Yes: distance, books, study time, counts or a unit of your own. Time can be measured with a timer and logged directly.

What does Tsumiage Pro add?

Unlimited goals, recurring deposit reminders, every chart and widget style, detailed analysis, Face ID lock and CSV export. Family Sharing is supported.
